How to Scrutinize Candidates & Manifestos

Avoid sensationalism by evaluating political platforms across 5 crucial pillars.

An election manifesto is a formal contract between a candidate and the public. Before casting your vote, benchmark each candidate's promises against these factual standards:

🏥 Healthcare & Wellbeing

  • Specific plans to lower out-of-pocket costs and drug prices.
  • Investment in local clinics, emergency response, and mental health.

💼 Economy & Cost of Living

  • Concrete measures for wage growth, inflation mitigation, and local jobs.
  • Clear budgetary proposals indicating where funding originates.

🎓 Education & Youth Pathways

  • Public school funding, teacher retention, and technical apprenticeships.
  • Relief strategies for vocational training and higher education costs.

🌱 Energy & Infrastructure

  • Modernizing power grids, clean water systems, and local transit.
  • Actionable timelines for carbon reduction and climate resilience.

⚖️ Rule of Law & Civil Rights

  • Commitment to protecting fair voting access and judicial independence.
  • Public disclosure of campaign donors and criminal background records.

Your Inviolable Voting Rights & Protections

Never leave a polling booth without casting your vote. Know your legal entitlements.

1. In Line by Closing Time

If you are in line before poll closing time (e.g. 7 PM or 8 PM), election laws state officials must allow you to vote. Stay in line!

2. Provisional / Tendered Ballot

If poll workers cannot find your name, you have the statutory right to cast a Provisional Ballot (US HAVA Sec 302) or Tendered Vote (India Rule 49P).

3. Absolute Ballot Secrecy

No election worker, employer, or family member can view your vote. Campaigning within the designated exclusion zone (usually 100 feet) is illegal.

4. Disability & Language Assistance

You have the legal right to receive assistance from a person of your choice (other than your employer or union boss), or use accessible audio/tactile machines.
